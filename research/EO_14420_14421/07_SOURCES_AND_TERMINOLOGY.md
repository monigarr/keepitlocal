## Terminology

### Bulk-power system

For this Executive Order, the term includes facilities and control systems necessary for an interconnected transmission network and generation needed for reliability. The order specifies transmission lines rated at 69 kV or higher.

### Bulk-power-system electric equipment

The order lists a broad set of generation, transmission, substation, protection, control, storage, and related systems. It also permits consideration of associated software, firmware, remote-access capabilities, lifecycle maintenance/update mechanisms, and supply-chain dependencies.

### Foreign-produced

The order defines this as an article that is not manufactured, produced, or assembled in the United States.

### Covered Foreign Entity

A country or person meeting the order's specified relationship to a foreign government and sanctions/arms-embargo criteria, or otherwise designated under the order's implementation process.

### Critical infrastructure

A broad term for systems whose disruption could materially affect public safety, economic activity, government functions, or other essential services. Do not assume that every AI/data-center component is legally classified as critical infrastructure.

### Sovereign AI

In this repository, the term means AI capability designed to preserve meaningful control over models, data, compute, infrastructure, governance, or deployment according to the needs of the entity/community operating it.

It does not necessarily mean complete independence from all external services.

## Research cautions

### EO numbering

The supplied White House page labels the August 26 action **Executive Order 14420**. Other public references may use **14421**. This repository deliberately uses both in the folder name until the discrepancy is reconciled through authoritative publication records.

### Jurisdiction

The U.S. Executive Order is not a general law governing Akwesasne as a whole.

Akwesasne is a cross-border community. Each infrastructure question must be analyzed according to the jurisdiction and ownership of the specific infrastructure.

### Data-center conclusions

Do not infer that:

- large electrical demand = national-security threat;
- foreign manufacture = malicious equipment;
- federal emergency = project prohibition;
- federal policy = Indigenous jurisdiction;
- state moratorium = permanent ban.

Those are separate questions.

## Evidence labels

Use these labels consistently:

- **CONFIRMED** — primary evidence.
- **REPORTED** — credible secondary reporting.
- **INFERRED** — technical inference.
- **UNKNOWN** — not established by available evidence.
