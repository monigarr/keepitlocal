# Keep It Local — Principle: Sovereign AI

## What it means

Sovereign AI means AI capability designed to preserve **meaningful control over the models, data, compute, infrastructure, governance, or deployment** used by the entity or community operating it.

It does **not** mean complete independence from all external services.

It also does **not** require building a hyperscale data center.

## Why it matters

Modern AI infrastructure can be heavily centralized — centralized compute, platforms, data, models, power consumption, supply chains, and control. Centralization can be efficient; it also creates dependency.

For many individuals, organizations, and communities, sovereign AI can mean:

- local models;
- local inference;
- local storage;
- local backups;
- local networking;
- local knowledge bases;
- controlled access;
- community governance;
- appropriate offline capability.

## Knowledge and language control

Community-controlled AI is especially important where data contains language knowledge, cultural knowledge, oral histories, community records, or locally generated datasets.

The governing idea:

> **The people who generate and steward the knowledge should have meaningful control over the infrastructure through which that knowledge is stored, processed, and used.**

## Design principle

> **Use the cloud when it makes sense. Keep what is essential close to home. Know the difference.**
